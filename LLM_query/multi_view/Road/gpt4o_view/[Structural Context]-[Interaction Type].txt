cavity: A disruptive void that breaks the continuity of the surrounding layered structure.

crack: A disruptive void that appears as a planar break within the layered structure.

loose: A textural replacement where the original, coherent layered structure is substituted with a disorganized, chaotic zone.

normal: The baseline structure itself, representing the coherent, layered background.

pipeline: An embedded object that is situated within the baseline structure.
