cavity: A very high-amplitude reflection at its upper boundary, creating a bright, high-contrast signature.

crack: A high-amplitude, sharp reflection that stands out clearly from the background layers.

loose: Moderate to low-amplitude, non-uniform reflections with a low-contrast, noisy texture.

normal: Low to moderate-amplitude background reflections with no localized high-amplitude events.

pipeline: A localized high-amplitude reflection that forms a distinct, high-contrast curve.
